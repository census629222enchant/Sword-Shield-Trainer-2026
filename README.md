## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Sword & Shield Simulator**. This trainer supports the game's blacksmithing and exploration systems with configurable options for resources, currency, crafting progression, inventory management, and skill development.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with blacksmithing, explore the medieval world freely, speed up progression, test crafting combinations, or reduce resource and currency management.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Launch the game and trainer separately, then activate movement-related options only after the game is fully loaded. Fast travel remains tied to discovered shrines, while the trainer can help reduce downtime between regions. Disable active options before loading a new area.

### Tools & Inventory

Use inventory options after opening the character or storage screen. Protect materials, weapons, shields, ore, wood, and other gathered items while moving resources between the forge, cart, storage, and merchants. Avoid changing item quantities during an active transaction.

### World & Environment

Explore Danburg, the Bloodlands, Grainmill, battlefields, forests, settlements, interiors, and hidden locations normally. Trainer options can reduce the cost of gathering, trading, contracts, and travel while leaving story missions, side quests, collectibles, lockpicking, and exploration available.

### Combat & Weapons

Sword & Shield Simulator focuses on blacksmithing, exploration, and story progression rather than direct player combat. Use crafting-related options to produce and improve swords, shields, armor-related equipment, and other forged goods used throughout the game's war-torn setting.
